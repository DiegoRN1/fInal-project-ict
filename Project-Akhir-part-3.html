How It Works

The RC car works using wireless communication between the remote control and the receiver module. When a button or joystick is pressed, a signal is sent to the controller. The controller then activates the motors to move the car forward, backward, left, or right.

Video
Diagram
How to Control

Use the remote control or smartphone controller to operate the car. The directional buttons or joystick send signals to the receiver, allowing the car to move and turn smoothly. The speed and direction depend on the commands given by the user.
