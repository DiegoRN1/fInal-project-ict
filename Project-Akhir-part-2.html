Game Explanation

Flappy Bird

Controls: Click to fly and avoid the pipes.

Objective: Reach the highest score possible without crashing.

Win/Lose: Win by getting a high score; lose if you crash into a pipe or fall.

Space Shooter

Controls: Use the arrow keys to move, spacebar to shoot.

Objective: Destroy enemies and survive as long as possible.

Win/Lose: Win with a high score; lose if your health is depleted.

Maze Game

Controls: Use the arrow keys or W-A-S-D to move.

Objective: Find the exit and get all the stars

Win/Lose: Reach the exit and get all the stars to win; lose if you touch purple block
