Bandung

Bandung is a city worth visiting because it offers a creative atmosphere and a wide selection of interesting attractions. Explore the Braga area, featuring old buildings and art galleries. Bandung is also known for its factory outlet shopping centers, easily found along several main streets. Local dishes like batagor and mie kocok enhance the travel experience.

Jakarta

As the capital city, Jakarta offers a vibrant, vibrant city experience every day. You can see the tall buildings in Sudirman, representing the central business district. Kota Tua offers a historical atmosphere with museums and old buildings. The National Monument (Monas) is a symbol of the city with its expansive park area.

Yogyakarta

Yogyakarta is a city worth visiting because its cultural life is evident in many places. You can see batik and silver crafts made directly by artisans. The palace (Keraton) demonstrates tradition with its regular daily activities. Malioboro offers a bustling atmosphere with vendors selling local products.
